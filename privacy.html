Privacy Policy

SilverTigerDev ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information regarding our mobile application 'DragonCat' (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal information from users of the App. However, the App integrates third-party services that may automatically collect certain device and usage information for advertising and analytics purposes.

  • Google AdMob: This service automatically collects data such as your mobile device's Advertising ID (e.g., Android GAID), device model, OS version, and ad interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

2. How Data is Used

The information automatically collected by third-party SDKs is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To deliver, personalize, and measure the effectiveness of advertisements.
  • To prevent fraud, monitor for invalid traffic, and ensure compliance with Google AdMob policies.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services used within the App. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of our third-party provider:

  • Google AdMob & Privacy Terms

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect or store your personal data directly, we do not retain any of your information. Data collected by third parties is retained and deleted in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Your Rights and Choices (GDPR/CPRA)

Users in certain regions, including the European Economic Area (EEA), have specific rights under data protection laws. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your mobile device settings:

  • Android: Settings -> Google -> Ads -> Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
